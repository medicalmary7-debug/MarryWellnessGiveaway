# Medical Mary 7-Day Wellness Reset Giveaway — Official Rules

**Promoter:** Medical Mary, 10 Kloof, Observatory, Johannesburg, South Africa.

These rules govern the Medical Mary 7-Day Wellness Reset giveaway. They should be reviewed by a South African legal or compliance professional before the campaign is publicly launched.

## 1. Promotional period

The giveaway opens on **Sunday, 27 September 2026 at 12:00 South African Standard Time (SAST)** and closes on **Saturday, 3 October 2026 at 12:00 SAST**. Entries received after the closing time will not be considered.

## 2. Eligibility

The giveaway is open to natural persons who are **18 years of age or older** and who live in Observatory, Johannesburg, or the surrounding areas, subject to any applicable legal restrictions. Employees of Medical Mary, their immediate families, and anyone professionally connected with the administration of this giveaway may be excluded at the promoter’s discretion.

The promoter may request reasonable information to verify eligibility before confirming a winner.

## 3. How to enter

To enter, an eligible participant must complete and submit the official Medical Mary entry form during the promotional period, confirm that they are at least 18 years old, and accept the official rules and privacy notice. **No purchase is required.**

Only **one entry per person** is permitted. Entries that are incomplete, duplicated, submitted after the closing time, or reasonably believed to be fraudulent may be disqualified.

The official entry form is hosted through Tally and is linked from the Medical Mary giveaway landing page.

## 4. Prize

One eligible winner will receive Medical Mary’s curated **Wellness, Your Way** package with a stated total value of **R700**, comprising the prize items described on the campaign page and subject to applicable product and legal restrictions.

The prize is not transferable, exchangeable for cash, or refundable. No substitution will be made unless the promoter is unable to provide an advertised item, in which case a reasonably equivalent alternative may be provided subject to applicable law.

The winner must collect the prize from **Medical Mary, 10 Kloof, Observatory, Johannesburg**. The promoter will confirm collection arrangements directly with the verified winner. Delivery is not included unless the promoter agrees otherwise in writing.

## 5. Winner selection

After entries close, the promoter will review the submissions and remove entries that are ineligible, incomplete, duplicated, late, or fraudulent. The winner will then be selected by a **random draw from the eligible entries**.

The promoter will keep a record of the draw date, the number of eligible entries, the selected entry, and any alternate entries drawn.

## 6. Contacting the winner

The potential winner will be contacted using the email address provided in the entry form. The potential winner will have **five calendar days** from the first contact attempt to respond and complete the eligibility and prize-acceptance process.

If the potential winner cannot be reached, does not respond within five calendar days, is ineligible, or declines the prize, the promoter may select and contact an alternate eligible entry.

The prize will only be confirmed after eligibility has been verified.

## 7. Personal information

Personal information submitted through the entry form will be used to administer the giveaway, verify eligibility, contact the potential winner, and manage prize collection. Optional marketing consent is separate from entry eligibility. Personal information will be handled in accordance with the promoter’s privacy notice and applicable South African data-protection law.

Participants should not submit passwords, payment-card details, banking PINs, or other unnecessary sensitive information through the entry form or social media.

## 8. Publicity

The promoter will not publish a winner’s name, photograph, or other identifying information without the winner’s appropriate consent, unless disclosure is required by law or permitted by the final approved rules and privacy notice.

## 9. Social-media platforms

This promotion is not sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, or any other social-media platform. Participants release those platforms from responsibility for the promotion to the extent permitted by the applicable platform terms.

## 10. General

The promoter may disqualify an entry or amend, suspend, or cancel the promotion where reasonably necessary because of fraud, technical failure, legal requirements, or circumstances outside the promoter’s reasonable control, subject to applicable law.

The final campaign, prize description, entry requirements, and personal-information wording should be reviewed and approved before public launch.
